Frequently Asked

Questions teams bring to the first call, answered in writing.

Thirty questions across six categories — engagement, pricing, process, AI agents, hiring developers, and security. Each answer is the one we would give on a sales call, written down so you can read it before we talk.

How do we start working with Vybog Services?
A 30-minute discovery call with a senior expert — no salesperson involved. If the fit looks right, we propose a Discovery Sprint (one to two weeks, fixed price) that ends with a written memo and an architecture sketch. From there we either move into a full engagement or you take the memo and use it elsewhere — no obligation either way.
What is the smallest engagement Vybog Services will take?
A 40-hour Hire-a-Developer engagement, or a fixed-price one-week Discovery Sprint. We do not take ad-hoc bug-fix work below 40 hours — the onboarding overhead makes it bad value for both sides.
Do you sign NDAs before the first call?
Yes. Mutual NDA on request, executed within one business day. Our standard NDA is available on request — most clients sign it without redlining.
Can we start small and expand if it works?
That is the most common pattern. Roughly two-thirds of our engagements start as a single Hire-a-Developer or one-sprint Discovery, and expand into a dedicated pod once trust is established. There is no contractual penalty for staying small.
What is the typical engagement length?
Hire-a-Developer: 3–6 months on average, often renewed. Dedicated pod: 6–18 months. Project engagements: 8–14 weeks. AI agent retainers: 12 months. We have clients who have been with us for over a decade and others we finished with in three weeks. Both are normal.
How does Vybog Services price engagements?
Three models. Hourly for Hire-a-Developer engagements (mid: $28–35/hr; senior: $40–55/hr; principal: $65–90/hr). Monthly for dedicated pods (fixed, prorated for partial months). Milestone-paid for fixed-scope projects (typically 30/30/30/10 split across kickoff, mid-build, validation, and 30-day post-launch acceptance).
Are rates negotiable for longer engagements?
Modestly, yes. Engagements over six months get a 5–10% discount on hourly rates; engagements over twelve months get 10–15%. We do not discount below cost — if a quoted rate feels too high it is more useful to discuss scope than price.
Do you charge for the discovery call?
No. The initial 30-minute discovery call is free. If you want a structured paid Discovery Sprint after that — one to two weeks, fixed price ($4,000–$8,000 depending on complexity) — you get a written memo, architecture sketch, and cost estimate to take wherever you want.
What are your payment terms?
Net-15 by default, billed monthly. We accept USD wire, USD ACH, INR bank transfer, and Wise. For first engagements with new clients, we ask for a 25% advance against the first month's invoice, refundable if you cancel within 14 days.
Are there any hidden costs?
No. Third-party services (cloud hosting, paid APIs, SaaS tools) are billed at cost with no markup, and only when you have approved them in writing in advance. Travel is billed only if you ask for it. We do not have a "project management overhead" line on our invoices.
What is the five-phase method?
Discover → Architect → Build → Validate → Launch & Iterate. Each phase has a written exit deliverable and an exit gate. The full long-form is on the process page.
How do you handle scope changes mid-engagement?
Every change is documented in a one-paragraph change order with impact on cost and timeline. You sign off in writing. No verbal scope expansion. We log every change order and review them in the next sprint retro — patterns that keep showing up usually mean the original scope was wrong, and we want to learn from that.
What does a typical week look like?
Daily written async standup in a shared Slack channel, mid-sprint check-in on day 5, code-freeze on day 9, live demo on day 10, retro on day 10 afternoon, backlog grooming on day 11. Two-week sprints. The full table is on the process page.
Who owns the code we write?
You do, on full payment of the milestone or month in which the code was written. We retain rights to our pre-existing internal libraries (Vybog Services Tools), which you get a perpetual licence to use within the deliverable. The full IP language is in the Terms of Service.
Will we always have the same expert?
Same expert by default. We do not rotate experts mid-engagement without your written consent. If an expert resigns, we provide a one-week paid handover and a replacement within seven business days.
How long does an AI agent project take?
Discovery sprint: 1–2 weeks. POC: 3–4 weeks. Production build: 6–10 weeks. So a typical end-to-end AI agent build runs 10–16 weeks. The first production agent often ships within four weeks if the use case is well-scoped.
Can the agent run entirely on-premise?
Yes. We have shipped agents that never call a public model API — using a self-hosted Llama or Mistral instance behind your firewall. The trade-off is model quality and operational overhead, both of which we will explain plainly during discovery.
How do you measure agent quality?
We write an eval suite in week one — a set of input cases with expected outputs, scored by a mix of automated checks (string match, JSON-shape, semantic similarity) and an LLM-as-judge with a rubric. The agent only ships when the eval pass rate clears the threshold you sign off on.
What happens when a foundation model is deprecated?
Our agents are written against an abstraction layer, so swapping models is a one-day rather than one-month exercise. We re-run the eval suite against the new model, surface any regressions, and roll forward only if pass rates hold. You see all of this in writing.
How are hallucinations and unsafe outputs handled?
Three layers. Input layer: prompt-injection detection, PII redaction. Reasoning layer: retrieval grounding for factual claims, citations required for any non-trivial assertion. Output layer: a separate model or rule-based classifier flags unsafe content before it leaves the agent. None of this is bulletproof — but it is honest.
How fast can we onboard a developer?
Seven business days from signed engagement to first day of work, in most cases. For specialized stacks (Rust, Elixir, niche game engines) or principal-level seniority, allow 10–14 business days.
Are these Vybog Services employees or contractors?
Full-time Vybog Services employees. We do not source from gig marketplaces. Every expert on our bench has been through our hiring panel and has a named tech lead behind them.
Can we interview the candidate?
Yes — we send three profiles, you screen, you interview, you pick. We pay for the interview time on our side. If none of the three fit, we resubmit at no charge.
What if the expert is not a good fit?
14-day no-fault replacement window. Tell us, we replace within seven business days, no offboarding fee. After 14 days, replacement still happens — it just goes through a written review so we both learn from the mismatch.
Can we hire them away from Vybog Services permanently?
After a minimum 12-month engagement, yes — at a one-time conversion fee equal to 20% of the expert's annual salary. Before 12 months, the standard non-solicit applies. Most clients renew the engagement instead of converting because the Vybog Services tech lead and back-bench come included.
What is your security posture?
SOC2 Type II-aligned controls (formal certification in progress). All expert machines are MDM-managed with full-disk encryption, screen-lock policies, automatic patch enforcement, and a written acceptable-use policy. All access to client systems goes through SSO with hardware MFA where supported.
Where is client data stored?
In your systems, by default. Vybog Services experts access your data through your accounts on your infrastructure — we do not export, mirror, or store client data on Vybog Services-controlled storage unless you ask us to.
How are credentials handled?
All credentials live in your secrets manager (1Password, Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, etc.) — never in code, never in chat, never in plain-text files. We never receive long-lived credentials by email or messaging tools.
Do you offer a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)?
Yes. We can execute a DPA aligned with GDPR Art. 28, with Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers, before any project work begins. India DPDP and CCPA-CPRA-aligned addenda available on request.
What happens at the end of an engagement?
Within 30 days: all Vybog Services access to your systems is revoked, all temporary working copies of your data are wiped, and you receive a written certification of deletion signed by the engagement lead. Any final invoices are settled before access revocation begins.
