Featured · Healthcare · 3-year engagement

Candorcare — caregiver matching for 14 US states.

A multi-tenant scheduling, billing, and Electronic Visit Verification platform serving non-medical home care agencies across 14 US states. Vybog Services built it end-to-end on Laravel and Vue, and continues to ship features behind weekly demos.

Outcomes after three years

  • 11,200+ caregivers active on the platform monthly
  • EVV compliance across all 14 deployed states, no fines or write-downs
  • Time-to-fill open shifts down from 6.2 hours to 22 minutes
  • Payroll-run time reduced from 11 hours to 38 minutes
