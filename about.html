Home/About Vybog
Founded 2002 · Chennai & Santa Clara

Twenty-two years of shipping software. Three years of shipping it with agents.

Vybog Services is a senior engineering studio built across two time zones. We have been writing production software since the year PHP 4 came out — and we are still doing it, with a steadier hand and a sharper toolkit, in 2026.

Our Story

Started as a two-person shop in Chennai. Stayed that careful as it grew.

In 2002 we registered as Vyinger Technologies in a single-room office in Valasaravakkam, Chennai. Two engineers, one printer, and a contract to rebuild a legacy classifieds site in PHP 4. We delivered on time. The client referred us to two more. That word-of-mouth chain — never broken, just slower or faster depending on the year — is still how most of our work shows up.

By 2008 we were thirty people. We had survived the dot-com hangover, lived through Web 2.0, and learned the unglamorous craft of supporting software you wrote three years ago. We rebranded to Vybog in 2010 — short, easy to pronounce in any accent, and free of the legacy associations of our first name.

In 2014 we opened our first US presence in Santa Clara, California — initially just a one-desk address for client invoicing. By 2019 it had grown into a full account-management and solution-architecture team, anchored by partners who had moved from the Chennai office to be closer to American customers.

The last three years have been our most material change since the rebrand. We were among the first studios in our peer group to build a dedicated AI agent practice — not as a side project, but as a discipline with its own delivery lead, its own evaluation methodology, and its own toolchain. Today AI agent work makes up close to forty percent of our revenue, and growing.

We are part of vy ventures, the holding entity for our family of brands. We answer to our clients first, our engineers second, and our holding company third — in that order, in writing, and the system has not yet broken.

What We Believe

Five opinions we'll defend in writing.

Not generic values. Opinions — the kind that shape what code we ship and what work we turn down.

Software is mostly maintenance.

The first version of any system is the easiest to build and the cheapest to discard. The system you operate in year three is what determines whether the project was worth doing. We architect for that future, even when the kickoff slide doesn't ask us to.

Senior beats junior, every time.

We do not staff projects with a senior architect at sales and a junior pool at delivery. The engineer pitching your project is the engineer writing your code. We charge a little more and ship a lot more on schedule.

AI is a tool, not a religion.

We use Cursor, Copilot, and Claude every day. We also know when not to. When a problem is not an AI problem — when it is a process problem, a data problem, or a leadership problem — we will tell you so, and lose the upsell.

Boring tech wins more bets.

PostgreSQL solves problems we have not had yet. Laravel still ships products. React is not dead. We will choose the framework that has five years of survivability left in it, not the one that trended on X last week.

The bill should match the demo.

Surprise change orders are a sign that someone wrote a bad SOW — usually us. We absorb that cost. Our retention rate of 92% is not luck; it is the result of refusing to nickel-and-dime through scope grey zones.

Two time zones is a feature.

Chennai-Santa Clara is not a compromise we put up with — it is the operating model we picked deliberately. You get senior engineering at fair rates with live US-Pacific overlap. The split is engineered, not improvised.

Holding Brand

A vy ventures company.

Vybog Services is one of several brands under vy ventures, an India-headquartered holding company building a portfolio of engineering, design, and AI-adjacent services. Each brand operates with its own P&L, its own delivery lead, and its own client roster. We share standards, not roadmaps.
