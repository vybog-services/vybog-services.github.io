How to engage Vybog Services

Pick the shape of the engagement. Not the other way around.

Four ways to commission Vybog Services — from one expert on your standup to a full delivery centre running your roadmap. Each model has a real price, a clear scope, and contract terms we negotiate in plain English.

In Depth

What each model actually looks like in practice.

Model 1

Hire a Developer

A single senior Vybog Services expert joins your standup, your repos, and your sprint board. They report into your tech lead, push code to your branches, and behave like any other member of your team — except they roll off cleanly when the project does.

Who picks this

Founders with a tech lead in-house who need extra capacity. Delivery managers covering a parental leave. Teams running a 90-day push to ship something specific.

What you get

  • One senior expert (8+ yrs typical), your choice of stack
  • 4–6 hours of live US-Pacific overlap, full EMEA overlap
  • 14-day mutual replacement window if the fit is wrong
  • NDA and IP assignment in place before day one
Model 2 · Most popular

Hire a Dedicated Team

A 3–8 person Vybog Services pod — experts, QA, designer, tech lead — focused exclusively on your roadmap. A Vybog Services delivery lead is accountable end-to-end for shipping. Your team owns the product; ours owns the delivery.

Who picks this

Product teams shipping continuously. Companies whose roadmaps run faster than their hiring funnel. Anyone who has watched a "staff augmentation" arrangement collapse into chaos.

What you get

  • Dedicated pod, your priorities, your repos, your CI/CD
  • Vybog Services delivery lead managing standups, sprints, demos
  • Fixed monthly cost — predictable burn for finance
  • Quarterly retros and roadmap planning with your leadership
Model 3

Offshore Delivery Centre (ODC)

A multi-pod, multi-discipline extension of your experting organisation — branded with your name on the floor, governed by your standards, but operating out of our Chennai facility. We provide infrastructure, HR, payroll, real estate, and continuous recruitment.

Who picks this

Mid-market and enterprise organisations running 12+ month programmes. Companies that want offshore scale without setting up an Indian subsidiary themselves.

What you get

  • 10 to 40+ experts, designers, QA, DevOps in one place
  • Engagement manager, delivery leads per pod, dedicated recruiter
  • Your security policies enforced on Vybog Services hardware
  • Visit your team in Chennai — they all sit on one floor
Model 4

Fixed-Bid Project

Discovery, statement of work, milestone-paid delivery of a defined scope. We commit to a date, a deliverable, and a number. You commit to acceptance criteria and signing off milestones within five business days.

Who picks this

Teams who need a deliverable on a date with a number on it. Procurement-driven organisations that can only buy projects, not capacity. Anyone burned by time-and-materials sprawl.

What you get

  • Written SOW with clear acceptance criteria per milestone
  • Milestone billing — no surprise change orders inside scope
  • Includes one quarter of post-launch support by default
  • Scope-change process documented, costed, time-boxed
Pricing Models

Four ways the invoice gets written.

Different engagements line up with different billing rhythms. We pick what fits — and we never run a model the contract didn't say.

Hourly

Logged in 15-minute increments, invoiced weekly with a per-expert breakdown. Best for short bursts, post-launch support, exploratory work.

Monthly Retainer

Predictable cost, predictable capacity. Best for embedded teams and dedicated pods. Unused hours roll over within the quarter.

Milestone-Based

Tied to defined deliverables in the SOW. Typically 20% kickoff, 30% mid-build, 40% staging, 10% post-launch warranty.

Outcome-Based

A portion of fee tied to a measurable outcome — uptime SLA, automation rate, conversion lift. We only do this when we control the variables.

Contracts in Plain English

NDAs and IP, without the lawyer fog.

The contract is one of the most expensive parts of a software engagement to get wrong. We have refined our standard MSA over twenty years and most clients sign it with minor tweaks.

NDA

Mutual NDA signed before the first scoping call. Covers conversations, code, data, and product roadmaps. Standard 5-year term. We'll sign your NDA template if you prefer — we are flexible on form, strict on intent.

IP Assignment

All work product is yours, by default, on full payment. Source code lives in your repositories from day one. Background IP (frameworks, internal libraries that pre-date your project) is licensed to you in perpetuity but remains ours.

Confidentiality

Experts sign individual confidentiality and IP-assignment agreements at the company level — your data is protected even if a Vybog Services expert moves on.
