Three agent shapes

RAG agents. Voice agents. Autonomous workflows.

"Build us an AI agent" usually means one of these three things. The architecture, the evals, and the on-prem story change accordingly.

RAG Knowledge Agents

Retrieval-augmented agents over your documents, tickets, code, or product data. Vector store + reranker + LLM, with eval harness from day one.

  • Pinecone / Qdrant / Weaviate
  • Reranker + chunking strategy
  • Eval pass rate > 85% threshold
Most common

Voice Agents

Phone-grade conversational agents for support, sales, scheduling. Sub-500ms latency, barge-in handling, intent classification, fallback to human.

  • STT/TTS pipeline tuning
  • <500ms agent response latency
  • Human handoff with state

Autonomous Workflows

Multi-step agents that read mail, draft documents, browse, hit APIs, ship work. Tool-use orchestration with guardrails and audit trails.

  • Tool-use orchestration
  • Human-in-the-loop checkpoints
  • Full audit trail of every action
Our AI Delivery Process

From discovery call to production agent in five phases.

The same five-step rhythm we use for software engagements, with eval gates built into every step.

1

Discovery

One to two weeks. We listen to a real conversation between the human currently doing the work and the user. That recording becomes our first eval set.

2

POC

Three to four weeks. End-to-end vertical slice — one task, one user, one tool integration. The point is to fail fast on the assumptions that matter.

3

Production Build

Six to ten weeks. Hardening: guardrails, prompt versioning, observability, fallback paths, human-in-the-loop hand-offs, security review.

4

Eval & Launch

The agent ships only after passing a written eval suite that you sign off on. Canary rollout, watchdog dashboards, on-call rotation for the first 30 days.

5

Continuous Tuning

Weekly review of failure cases, prompt patches, model swaps. Quarterly retrain or re-prompt depending on drift signals. You decide the cadence.

Two weeks, fixed price. We map your use case, score feasibility, write a target eval set, and hand you an architecture sketch you can take anywhere — even to a different vendor.

Agent Build & Launch

Fixed scope, milestone-paid. POC in four weeks, production in eight to twelve, post-launch tuning included for one quarter. Best when the use case is clear.

AI Pod Retainer

Dedicated 3–5 person AI pod — expert, ML, data, designer, lead. For organizations rolling out a portfolio of agents across functions over a year or longer.

  • Shared eval infrastructure across all agents
