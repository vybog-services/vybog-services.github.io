The marketing experting loop

Five steps. Two-week cadence. Documented every cycle.

This is how we operate inside the marketing pod, every sprint, regardless of channel.

1

Measure

Pull the data first. What did last sprint do? Where did the curve bend? Decide based on the numbers, not the calendar.

2

Hypothesize

Write the hypothesis in one sentence: "We believe X will move Y by Z." Anything fuzzier than that does not enter the backlog.

3

Ship

Launch the experiment behind a flag or a tagged campaign. Define the success metric and the sample size before traffic starts.

4

Validate

Read the result with a confidence threshold, not a hopeful eyeball. Ship the winner, kill the loser, log both.

5

Document

Every cycle ends with a one-page experiment doc in your shared drive. By month six you have a playbook nobody can lose.

SEO methodology

Four moves that still work in 2026.

The SEO landscape changed when AI overviews showed up. The fundamentals did not — but the priorities did.

Technical SEO

Crawl budget, indexability, structured data, Core Web Vitals — the foundation work that has to be green before any content investment pays back.

Topical clusters

Pillar pages plus a constellation of supporting articles, internal-linked deliberately. Authority comes from depth in a niche, not breadth across forty topics.

Programmatic SEO

Hundreds or thousands of templated long-tail pages, generated from your structured data — done with editorial guardrails, not as content sludge.

AI-overview optimization

Format content to be cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity — schema, factual density, and authoritative source signals.

Channel-specific playbooks

The short version, by channel.

Google Search

Branded + non-brand split discipline. Search query reports reviewed weekly, negative keyword lists trimmed monthly. We bid on intent, not keyword count.

  • Smart Bidding with offline conversion uploads from your CRM
  • Asset experiments running on a four-week rotation
  • Landing pages tested for the specific match-type cohort

LinkedIn

Account-based for B2B SaaS, sponsored content for thought leadership, document ads for downloadable assets. We avoid the platforms LinkedIn invents quarterly.

  • Account lists synced from Salesforce or HubSpot
  • Lead Gen Form fields kept under five for response rates
  • Conversation ads used sparingly and only with relevant offers

Meta

Best for DTC, app installs, and broad-funnel B2C. Advantage+ campaigns with a creative library deep enough to feed the algorithm what it wants.

  • 15+ creative concepts per campaign, refreshed every two weeks
  • Conversions API wired server-side; iOS 14.5+ impact mitigated
  • Lookalike audiences seeded from CRM-validated buyers

Email

Lifecycle flows that earn engagement instead of training users to delete. Triggered by behavior, scored by inbox placement, not just open rate.

  • Welcome → activation → renewal flows with branching logic
  • Deliverability monitoring: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI configured
  • Quarterly list hygiene to keep sender reputation high
