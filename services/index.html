First three weeks

How an engagement starts.

Before any code is written or any retainer signed, we run a short three-step intake. It costs you a few hours. It saves a quarter of mismatched expectations.

Day 0 – 3

Conversation

A 45-minute call with a senior engineer — not an account manager. We listen to the problem in your own words and ask the questions that surface the unsaid constraints. You leave with our written read of the brief.

Day 3 – 10

Scoping document

We send back a two-page scoping document: assumptions, recommended approach, suggested pod shape, and a price range with the cost drivers shown. No glossy pitch deck, no upsell.

Day 10 – 21

Discovery sprint or kickoff

For fixed-bid work we run a paid one-week discovery to validate the estimate. For team engagements we start onboarding within five business days of contract sign.

Three ways to buy

One delivery standard. Three commercial shapes.

The discipline you pick from the grid above is the what. The model below is the how. Mix and match as the project evolves — most clients start in one and migrate to another by month six.

By the hour

Embedded developer

A single Vybog engineer joining your standups, your repos, your Slack. Best when you already have a tech lead and need to add capacity without adding a hire.

  • One senior engineer, your choice of stack
  • Hourly or monthly billing, prorated
  • 14-day mutual replacement window
By the month

Dedicated pod

A 3 – 8 person pod — engineering, QA, design, tech lead — focused exclusively on your roadmap. The most common shape for growing product teams.

  • Vybog delivery lead end-to-end
  • Fixed monthly cost, predictable burn
  • Weekly demo, monthly retro, quarterly QBR
By the milestone

Fixed-scope project

A statement of work with defined deliverables and acceptance criteria. Best when the build is bounded and you need a price on a date.

  • Milestone-based invoicing
  • No surprise change orders
  • One quarter of post-launch support included

When problems cross pillars

Most real builds touch three of these at once.

A B2B SaaS launch is rarely just "software development." It needs a marketing site, a customer portal, lifecycle email, sometimes an AI agent for support deflection — and it all needs to ship on the same date.

Vybog's practice leads sit on the same floor in Chennai and share a single delivery lead per client. When your engagement spans pillars, you do not get four account managers. You get one named owner who runs the whole thing, with the right specialists pulled in week by week.
