The mobile experience stack

Six things that separate a Vybog Services app from a Vybog Services-shaped wrapper.

UI motion

Spring-based transitions, gesture-driven dismissals, and 60fps scroll on the cheapest device your audience actually owns — not just on the demo iPhone.

Offline-first data

Local SQLite or Realm cache with conflict-resolution on sync. Your users keep working on a flight, in a basement, or on a flaky cellular link.

Push & background sync

APNs, FCM, OneSignal — wired with payload versioning and silent push for background refresh, without burning the battery the user is paying attention to.

Biometric auth

Face ID, Touch ID, Android BiometricPrompt with a sensible fallback ladder, secure enclave for tokens, and a session policy your security review will pass.

Deep links & universal links

App-link manifests verified at the apex domain, deferred deep links for first-time installs, and analytics attribution that survives the App Store handoff.

In-app purchases & subscriptions

StoreKit 2, Google Play Billing, RevenueCat — receipt validation server-side, restore flows tested in sandbox before the App Review team finds them.

App store launch playbook

Five stages between "feature complete" and "live in the store."

Most app delays happen in the last three weeks, not the first three months. We run this rhythm on every release.

1

Internal & TestFlight

Daily builds to internal testers and TestFlight / Play Internal track. Smoke tests, accessibility passes, and battery-burn checks before external eyes.

2

Closed beta

50 – 200 real users invited under NDA. Crashlytics dashboards reviewed daily; a feedback survey lands in week one and again at the close.

3

Submission prep

Screenshots in five locales, store listing copy, privacy nutrition label, App Privacy Report fields, age rating, export compliance — checklist-driven.

4

Review & approval

We respond to Apple / Google reviewer questions within two business hours. Median time-to-approval on Vybog Services submissions: 2.4 days.

5

Phased rollout

Staged release at 1% → 10% → 50% → 100% with watchdog dashboards on crash rate and ANR. Auto-pause thresholds wired into Slack on day one.

Post-launch care

The bit most agencies pretend does not exist.

Bug-fix SLA

Sev-1 issues (crash on launch, payment failure) acknowledged in 2 business hours, hotfix submitted in 24. Sev-2 in five business days. Written into the SOW.

OS compatibility passes

iOS and Android beta SDKs tested against your app before public release each year. We pre-empt deprecations, not chase them.

Store listing & ASO
