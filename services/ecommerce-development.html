Platform migrations done quietly

The replatform you don’t feel in next quarter’s revenue.

Migrations are the highest-stakes work in commerce. Done badly they lose SEO traffic, break checkout for a week, scramble customer records and erase loyalty balances. Done with discipline they ship over a weekend and the only metric that moves is page-load.

1 · Discovery & audit

We crawl the existing storefront, map every URL with traffic, every active checkout funnel, every payment gateway, every customer-facing email template. The output is a written replatform memo that names the rough-edges before contract signature, not after the kick-off call.

2 · Data & SKU mapping

Every product, variant, tax class, customer record, order history, subscription, gift-card balance and loyalty point gets mapped to the target schema. Edge cases (composite SKUs, configurable bundles, B2B price lists, multi-warehouse stock) are scoped explicitly — not handed to the import script and hoped for.

3 · SEO preservation plan

Before any code moves, we write the 301 redirect map (covering category slugs, faceted-search URLs, AMP variants, legacy session-id formats), the canonical strategy, the schema.org rebuild, the sitemap regeneration cadence, and the Search Console handoff. Organic traffic typically recovers within two Googlebot crawl cycles of go-live.

4 · Staging, parity testing & UAT

The new storefront runs in a production-shaped staging environment with real anonymised data, synthetic checkout transactions, payment-gateway sandboxes and tax calculations cross-checked against the old system. We run a parity dashboard until the two stores agree on every line item.

5 · Cutover weekend

Maintenance window, final data sync, DNS flip, 301 verification, payment gateway re-binding, customer notification email, ops war-room on Slack. Two senior Vybog Services leads on-call from Friday night through Tuesday morning. Rollback path documented and rehearsed before the window opens, not invented at 3am if something breaks.

6 · Post-launch stabilisation

30 days of post-cutover on-call included. We watch crawl coverage, conversion rate, checkout completion, refund volume, and payment-gateway error rate against the pre-migration baseline. Anything moving more than a defined threshold gets a written root-cause memo within 48 hours.

E-commerce engagement questions, answered before the call.

Quick answers to what most clients ask. Reach out if you do not see your question.

Can you migrate us from Magento 1 / Magento 2 / Shopify to a different platform?
Yes — this is the largest part of our 2025 e-commerce book. We’ve done Magento 1 → Shopify Plus, Magento 2 → commercetools (headless), Shopify Plus → BigCommerce B2B Edition, and bespoke PHP carts to WooCommerce. Each one starts with a written replatform memo so you know what you’re signing up for.
How long does a typical replatform take?
Discovery: 2–3 weeks. Build & data mapping: 8–14 weeks depending on catalogue complexity and B2B feature scope. Parity testing: 2–3 weeks. Cutover & stabilisation: a single weekend plus 30 days of on-call. Total: 12–20 weeks for most merchants.
Will we lose SEO traffic during the migration?
If the redirect map and canonical strategy are written before code starts, no — you’ll see a 5–15% organic dip in the first 14 days as Googlebot re-crawls, then recovery to baseline within four weeks. Our six 2025 replatforms averaged 11 days to recovery.
Do you handle PCI compliance and payment-gateway integration?
Yes. We work with Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, Razorpay, PayU, Klarna, Affirm, Afterpay, and the major regional gateways. PCI scope stays as tight as the platform allows — tokenised, hosted-fields or fully redirected checkout depending on your risk posture and which platform we’re on.
What does a maintenance retainer cost and cover?
Retainers start at 40 senior hours / month and scale to dedicated pods. Coverage includes platform patching, dependency updates, theme & content tweaks, A/B test implementation, observability, and peak-event readiness. A named senior on every account. Hours roll over inside the quarter, never lost.
Can you take over an existing store from a previous agency?
Routinely. We start with a 5-day audit — code quality, dependency posture, observability state, SEO health, integration plane. The audit produces a written triage memo and a stabilisation plan with hours estimated. Whether or not you continue with us after that is your call.
Do you build B2B and wholesale storefronts?
Yes. Per-customer price lists, net-payment terms, multi-buyer accounts, quote-to-cart flows, sales-rep impersonation, customer-specific catalogues, EDI integration where relevant. BigCommerce B2B Edition and Adobe Commerce B2B are our most common picks; Shopify Plus B2B is fine for simpler structures.
