How We Deliver

The rhythm that survives reality.

Every Vybog Services engagement — software, mobile, CMS, AI agent, or a single embedded expert — runs on the same five-phase method. This page is the long-form version. Read it before our first call so we can skip the procedural Q&A.

Book a discovery call → Engagement models
The Five-Phase Method

Five phases. Each with a deliverable. Each with an exit gate.

No phase ends because the calendar says so. Each ends because the next phase has what it needs to begin without guessing.

1 · Discover

Duration: 1–2 weeks. Exit deliverable: A written discovery memo, signed off by both sides.

We do four things in parallel: a stakeholder mapping exercise (who decides what), a user interview pass (three to six of your real users, recorded), a code-base or system-state read where one exists, and a constraint inventory (compliance, deadlines, budget envelope, immovables). The discovery memo names the problem in your words, our hypotheses about the solution shape, the assumptions we cannot defend yet, and a short list of decisions you will need to make before the next phase begins.

We do not produce a Gantt chart in this phase. Gantt charts produced without architecture decisions are creative writing.

2 · Architect

Duration: 1–3 weeks depending on scope. Exit deliverable: An architecture document, a data model, and a milestone-paid statement of work.

This is where we make the decisions that are expensive to undo. Data model first — entities, relationships, ownership boundaries, soft-delete and audit policy. Then API surface — resource shapes, error contracts, auth model, versioning policy. Then security boundaries — what data is sensitive, where it lives, who can see it under which conditions. Then observability — what we will measure, what triggers a page, how dashboards are organized. Then the diagram you will print and tape to the wall.

The exit deliverable also includes a milestone-paid statement of work — fixed price per milestone, change-order mechanics agreed in advance, no surprises during build.

3 · Build

Duration: Variable. Typical builds run 6–14 weeks. Exit deliverable: A feature-complete, instrumented, internally-demoed system.

Two-week sprints with a Tuesday demo to your team. Feature flags from the first commit so we can ship in halves. Instrumentation, logs, and traces from the first commit so we are never debugging blind. Pull requests reviewed by a second Vybog Services expert before they land — no exceptions, even for the senior lead.

Demo participants are encouraged to break things. If a demo passes silently it usually means we forgot to invite the person who should have raised the awkward question.

4 · Validate

Duration: 1–3 weeks. Exit deliverable: A validation report with five sub-reports plus a written sign-off from a named stakeholder on your side.

The five sub-reports are: unit and integration test coverage with a written explanation of what we deliberately did not test, a load test with results against a target traffic profile, a security scan (SAST, dependency vulnerability scan, manual code review of the auth and payment paths), an accessibility audit against WCAG 2.2 AA, and a UAT pass with a checklist signed by a non-experting stakeholder.

This phase is a gate, not a formality. We have delayed launches by two weeks more than once because the security scan turned up something that needed real work.

5 · Launch & Iterate

Duration: Open-ended. Default first-30-days on-call included. Exit deliverable: A 30-day post-launch report and a maintenance arrangement of your choice.

Staged rollout — internal users first, then a friendly 5% canary, then 25%, then everyone. Watchdog dashboards live during each step, with paging rules wired up to whoever is on the rotation. For the first 30 days post-launch, a Vybog Services expert is on call without extra billing, and we hold a weekly review to look at production data versus pre-launch hypotheses.

At the 30-day mark we hand you a written report — what worked, what surprised us, what we recommend changing in the next quarter. From there you decide: retainer, project-based extensions, or full hand-off.

Tools We Run Delivery On

The stack behind the engagements.

We bring our defaults. If you have working alternatives in place, we use yours — we are not religious about tools.

Linear & Jira

Linear for net-new engagements, Jira when your team already lives there. One board per engagement. We do not run a parallel Vybog Services-only board.

Slack Connect

A shared Slack Connect channel per engagement. Daily-active responses during your working hours, async during ours. No Slack DMs to individual experts — everything stays in-channel for continuity.

GitHub or your repos

Code lives in your repos, in your org. Vybog Services experts join under named accounts. CI on your runners or ours — your call.

Figma

Design lives in one Figma workspace per engagement. Component libraries flow back into the build phase as a token export.

Loom

Async demos are recorded for stakeholders who cannot attend the live demo. We aim to replace 60% of would-be meetings with a 5-minute Loom and a written summary.

Notion

Engagement wiki — discovery memo, architecture doc, runbooks, post-mortems, retros. You get a copy with every export at the end of the engagement.

Quality Gates

Five gates code passes before it reaches your users.

Bypass is allowed only with a written exception from your account. We log the exceptions and review them in the next retro.

Code review

Every PR reviewed by a second Vybog Services expert, with an inline rubric for security, performance, and readability.

Test coverage

Target depends on the code's risk class. Auth, payments, and data migration code get higher bars than CRUD admin views.

Security scan

SAST plus dependency vulnerability scan on every PR. Manual security review on every milestone.

Performance budget

Each user-facing path has a budget — Lighthouse, INP, cold-start, p95 latency — measured in CI on every PR.

Accessibility audit

WCAG 2.2 AA is the default bar for user-facing surfaces. Automated tooling plus a manual screen-reader pass at every milestone.

Communication Cadence

What you hear from us, when.

Three rhythms — none of which involve a 47-slide monthly status deck.

Daily

Written async standup

Posted by 11am your local time in the shared Slack channel. Three lines per expert — yesterday, today, blockers. Searchable later when you need to reconstruct what happened in a given week.

Weekly

Live sprint demo + check-in

30-minute video call. Demo first, questions next, decisions captured in writing inside the call. If you cannot attend live, we send a Loom recording the same day.

Monthly

Engagement review

A written one-pager from the Vybog Services delivery lead — burn rate, scope health, risks, decisions needed. You read it. We discuss only the items you flag.

Risk Management

What we do when reality stops cooperating.

Scope change

Every requested change is documented in a one-paragraph change order with impact on cost and timeline. You sign off in writing before anyone touches code. Verbal scope expansion does not happen quietly on a Vybog Services engagement.

Blockers

If an expert is blocked for more than four hours, the tech lead is paged. If still blocked at end of day, you are paged the next morning. We do not let work invisibly stall while the burn meter runs.

Replacement

Performance issues are handled with a written warning, a documented improvement plan, and a 14-day mutual replacement window with no offboarding fee. Resigning experts are replaced with a one-week handover.
