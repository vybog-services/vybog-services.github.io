AI-Native Delivery

Why we can do more, faster — without raising the price.

Your competitors hired one freelancer. You are hiring a team where AI does the routine 70% and a senior does the judgment 30%. The economics work in your favor.

AI writes the first draft. A senior approves what ships.

Site copy, product descriptions, blog posts, FAQs, support replies — AI gets the routine first pass done in minutes, not days. A human reviewer catches what is wrong, polishes what is right, and signs off. You see the final, not the rough draft.

AI watches your site at 3 AM. A human fixes what matters at 9.

Uptime, page-speed regressions, broken links, expired certificates, search-rank drops — the AI watcher catches it the moment it happens. If it can be auto-fixed, it is. If it needs judgment, a human looks at it the next morning. You hear about it once it is resolved.

AI handles routine questions. You handle the ones that earn loyalty.

Hours, location, menu, return policy, booking changes, basic troubleshooting — an AI agent answers these on your site, via email, and on chat. The 40% of questions that need a human reach you with full context. The other 60% never need to interrupt your day.

FAQ

Plain answers to the questions every owner asks first.

I do not have a website at all — where do we start?
Right at the beginning. We help you pick the domain, build the brand, write the copy, design the pages, set up Google Business Profile, and switch on an AI agent before the doors open. You do not need to know anything technical — that is what we do.
Do I need a logo, domain, Google listing — can you handle all of it?
Yes, end to end. Logo and brand book are part of the Web Identity service. Domain registration, secure email, and hosting are included in setup. Google Business Profile and Maps are claimed and optimized as part of every launch.
Wix or Squarespace looks cheap — why pay someone to do it for me?
Wix and Squarespace are great for hobby sites and personal portfolios. They struggle when you need real SEO, an actual store, custom integrations, or ongoing performance work. The honest answer: if your business is a side project, DIY is fine. If it is your livelihood, the time you save and the customers you keep usually pay for the difference within a quarter.
Why should I leave my current developer or web person?
You probably should not, if they are watching your site daily, shipping improvements proactively, and reporting on what changed. Most freelance relationships are built-once then break-fix — great for the launch, light on aftercare. We replace the aftercare part. You can keep working with your developer on bespoke projects if that fits.
What if I just want the website, without the monthly stuff?
That is fine. The Web Identity, E-commerce, and Migration services can be bought as one-time projects. We do recommend at least a minimal monthly care plan so the security patches, certificate renewals, and backups stay current — but it is not required to start.
Will I lose my current site, content, or data?
No. Migrations preserve your URLs, content, customer data, and search rankings. Your old site stays online until cutover, so there is no downtime window. We hand you a written export of everything on day one and again at any cancellation.
What if I am not technical at all?
Most of our customers are not. The whole point of bundled care is that you do not have to know what a DNS record or an SSL certificate is — we handle it. You stay focused on running your business.
Can I cancel? What happens to my site if I do?
Yes, with one month notice. On cancellation we hand you the source code, content export, customer data, and full access to your hosting and domain. Nothing is locked in. You walk away with everything you would have if you had built it yourself.
Do you work with Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress?
Yes, all of them. We pick the platform that fits your business, not the one we are most comfortable with. If you are already on a platform that works, we keep you there. If it is hurting you, we say so and propose a migration.
How does the AI customer support actually work in practice?
We build an AI agent that knows your menu, hours, policies, products, prices, FAQs — whatever is public on your site plus anything you upload (PDFs, internal docs, scripts). It answers chat, email, and form questions. Anything outside its rules gets escalated to you with full context. You review the logs weekly and update the rules as the business changes.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. Hosting is encrypted at rest and in transit. PCI compliance is handled by the payment processor (Stripe or your gateway of choice). We never store payment data on your servers. AI agents process customer queries with redaction rules you control — no customer PII leaves your platform without your explicit configuration.
