AI-Native Delivery

Why we do more, faster — without raising the price.

Your competitors hired one freelancer. You are hiring a team where AI does the routine 70% and a senior does the judgment 30%.

Most common

AI writes the first draft. A senior approves what ships.

Site copy, product descriptions, blog posts, FAQs, support replies — AI gets the routine first pass done in minutes, not days. A human reviewer catches what is wrong, polishes what is right, and signs off.

  • Brand-tuned tone guides written before any AI runs
  • Senior reviewer on every public surface before it ships
  • You see the final, never the rough draft

AI watches your site at 3 AM. A human fixes what matters at 9.

Uptime, page-speed regressions, broken links, expired certificates, search-rank drops — the AI watcher catches it the moment it happens. If it can be auto-fixed, it is. If it needs judgment, a human looks at it the next morning.

  • Synthetic checks every 60 seconds, 24/7
  • Auto-fix rules for routine outages (cert, cache, CDN)
  • Weekly written health-and-opportunity memo

AI handles routine questions. You handle the ones that earn loyalty.

Hours, location, menu, return policy, booking changes, basic troubleshooting — an AI agent answers these on your site, via email, and on chat. The 40% of questions that need a human reach you with full context.

  • Knowledge base sourced from your site + uploaded PDFs
  • Escalation rules you configure, logs you can audit
  • Multilingual where your customers actually live

AI surfaces growth ideas. You decide which to ship.

Page-level conversion analysis, slow-funnel detection, query-gap reports, headline-A/B suggestions — surfaced weekly with a one-line recommendation. You pick what gets built; we ship it under the same monthly plan.

  • Weekly opportunity memo, ranked by impact
  • Bundled implementation — no per-ticket quotes
  • Measured uplift, reported the following month

Common questions

Plain answers to the questions every owner asks first.

I do not have a website at all — where do we start?
Right at the beginning. We help you pick the domain, build the brand, write the copy, design the pages, set up Google Business Profile, and switch on an AI agent before the doors open. You do not need to know anything technical — that is what we do.
Do I need a logo, domain, Google listing — can you handle all of it?
Yes, end to end. Logo and brand book are part of the Web Identity service. Domain registration, secure email, and hosting are included in setup. Google Business Profile and Maps are claimed and optimized as part of every launch.
Wix or Squarespace looks cheap — why pay someone to do it for me?
Wix and Squarespace are great for hobby sites. They struggle when you need real SEO, an actual store, custom integrations, or ongoing performance work. If your business is a side project, DIY is fine. If it is your livelihood, the time you save and the customers you keep usually pay for the difference within a quarter.
Why should I leave my current developer or web person?
You probably should not, if they are watching your site daily, shipping improvements proactively, and reporting on what changed. Most freelance relationships are built-once then break-fix — great for the launch, light on aftercare. We replace the aftercare part. You can keep your developer on bespoke projects.
Will I lose my current site, content, or data?
No. Migrations preserve your URLs, content, customer data, and search rankings. Your old site stays online until cutover, so there is no downtime window. We hand you a written export of everything on day one and again at any cancellation.
Can I cancel? What happens to my site if I do?
Yes, with one month notice. On cancellation we hand you the source code, content export, customer data, and full access to your hosting and domain. Nothing is locked in. You walk away with everything you would have if you had built it yourself.
How does the AI customer support actually work in practice?
We build an AI agent that knows your menu, hours, policies, products, prices, FAQs — whatever is public on your site plus anything you upload. It answers chat, email, and form questions. Anything outside its rules gets escalated to you with full context. You review the logs weekly.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. Hosting is encrypted at rest and in transit. PCI compliance is handled by the payment processor. We never store payment data on your servers. AI agents process customer queries with redaction rules you control — no customer PII leaves your platform without your explicit configuration.
