Ruby on Rails hiring questions, answered before the call.

How fast can I hire Ruby on Rails developers from Vybog?
48 hours to a vetted shortlist, 5 working days to first commit. We have a bench of Rails engineers ready — not a sourcing funnel that starts after you sign.
What level of Ruby on Rails expertise do your developers have?
All Vybog Rails developers are senior (5+ years production Ruby on Rails). We do not staff junior engineers on client projects unless you specifically ask — and we will recommend against it.
Do I own the code your Rails developers write?
Yes — full IP transfer from the first commit. Code lives in your repository under your organization. We sign IP assignment as part of the MSA. No exceptions.
What if a Ruby on Rails developer is not the right fit?
14-day no-fault replacement window. If a developer is not working out in the first two weeks, we replace them at no charge and no offboarding fee.
Where are your Ruby on Rails developers based?
Distributed across Chennai (India) and Santa Clara (USA). Four hours of overlap with US Pacific, full overlap with EMEA. Your Slack thread doesn't die at 5pm your time.
Can I hire just one Ruby on Rails developer, or do I need a full pod?
One is fine. Our minimum engagement is a single embedded developer at 40 hours/month. You can scale up to a 6-person pod over time, or scale down with 30 days notice.
