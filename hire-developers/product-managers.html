Questions teams bring to the first call.

What is the difference between a Vybog PM and an interim VP Product?
Mostly seniority and scope. Our PMs run a single product line; the interim VP role coordinates across multiple PMs, handles hiring, runs roadmap review with the exec team.
Do your PMs run discovery and write PRDs themselves?
Yes — they own the artifacts they produce. PRDs are drafted in writing, reviewed by engineering, signed off in Confluence or Linear. No PowerPoint hand-waving.
Can our engineering team challenge the roadmap?
They should. Our PMs treat engineering as a partner, not a delivery shop. If eng pushes back on a scope decision, we have a written rationale to defend it or we update it.
