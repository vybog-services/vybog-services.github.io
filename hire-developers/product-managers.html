De-Risk the Hire

The two-week discovery sprint, before commitment.

If you are bringing in a Product Management product manager for a long engagement, the worst time to find out the fit is wrong is week six. Here is how we shrink that risk.

The sprint is a fixed two-week, fixed-fee engagement where one of our senior Product Management product managers reads your codebase, joins your standups, and ships a small but real PRD or experiment. At the end of the two weeks, both sides know enough to commit — or to walk away cleanly.

We bill the discovery sprint at a flat rate and credit it against the first month of any continuing engagement. If the fit is wrong, you keep the PRD or experiment, the documentation, and the architectural notes the expert wrote up. Nobody loses the quarter.

Product Management FAQ

Three questions we get on every Product Management engagement.

Quick answers to what most clients ask. Reach out if you do not see your question.

Will your PMs run user research themselves?
Yes. Every senior PM on the bench has run discovery interviews, synthesized findings, and turned them into PRDs. If a project warrants a dedicated researcher, we will tell you.
Do your PMs write SQL?
Yes. Comfortable in Amplitude / Mixpanel and in BigQuery / Postgres for ad-hoc analysis. Not a substitute for an analytics engineer, but enough to not block on one.
Roadmap, sprint planning, both?
Both. Roadmap as the strategic artifact, sprint planning as the operational rhythm. We will write the format that fits your team, not impose ours.
