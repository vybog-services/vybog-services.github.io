Definition

What "full-stack" actually means at Vybog.

Half the industry uses the label to mean "knows JavaScript on both sides." We use it more strictly — and we will tell you when your problem actually wants a specialist instead.

A Vybog full-stack developer owns five layers: the user-facing frontend (component architecture, accessibility, state, performance), the API tier (auth, routing, validation, error contracts), the data layer (schema design, query performance, migration discipline), light DevOps (Docker, CI, observability, a release that does not require the founder's WiFi), and the product instinct to know what to push back on. None of those layers requires a separate hire when the product is still finding its shape.

What we will not pretend a full-stack developer is: a data expert, an iOS specialist, a security researcher, or an SRE for a 100-million-row table under load. When the work crosses those lines, we say so, and we pair the full-stack lead with a specialist for the duration.

Stack Combinations

Six combos we field most often.

Each is a battle-tested pairing — not a list of frameworks we have a logo for.

MERN

MongoDB, Express, React, Node. Best for content-heavy products, marketplaces, and apps where the schema is going to keep moving.

Next.js + Node + Postgres

Our default for new builds in 2026. Strong typing, RSC where it earns its keep, Postgres because eventual-consistency surprises are expensive.

Django + React

For products with non-trivial admin workflows. Django's admin saves a quarter of internal-tooling time on its own.

Laravel + Vue / Inertia

For teams already invested in PHP, or rebuilding a legacy LAMP stack without throwing away the data model.

Rails + Hotwire

Founders shipping under a deadline who want to move at Rails speed without paying a separate frontend expert.

MEAN + TypeScript

Enterprise environments that already standardized on Angular and a Mongo backend. We will not argue with you.

Capability Window

Be honest about the ceiling.

Things a Vybog full-stack developer will own end-to-end — and things we will tell you to staff differently.

Can own end-to-end

  • Auth, user profile, role-based access
  • CRUD-heavy domain models with sensible indexing
  • Admin tooling, internal dashboards, reporting exports
  • Payments via Stripe / Razorpay including webhooks
  • Email lifecycle (transactional + marketing handoff)
  • File uploads, image pipelines, signed URLs
  • Search via Postgres FTS or a managed service (Algolia, Meilisearch)
  • Background jobs, scheduled work, queue ergonomics

Should pair with a specialist

  • High-traffic systems above 5k RPS on hot paths
  • Data-warehousing, ETL, near-realtime analytics pipelines
  • Native iOS or Android with deep platform integrations
  • ML training pipelines, model evaluation, fine-tuning
  • Security work that needs offensive testing or threat modelling
  • Design systems serving multiple product teams
  • SRE work involving Kubernetes at scale or chaos experting
Ideal Project Types

Four shapes of work we have seen succeed.

Early-stage MVPs

Founders who need to ship something investable in 8 to 12 weeks. One Vybog expert, one tech lead, weekly demo.

Internal tools

Ops dashboards, back-office workflows, the never-quite-built admin panel. Ships in 4 to 8 weeks usually.

Ongoing maintenance

A part-time expert keeping an inherited codebase healthy — patches, dependency upgrades, small features.

Founder-adjacent prototyping

Sitting next to a non-technical founder, turning Loom recordings into shippable code on a weekly cadence.

Frequently Asked

Three questions full-stack hires bring up.

How do you handle the moment a full-stack developer hits the ceiling of what they should own?
The Vybog tech lead on your account is checking for those signals during the weekly review — performance, security, or specialization work showing up in the backlog. When we see it, we surface it with you, and either pull in a Vybog specialist for a short engagement or recommend a permanent hire. We do not let the full-stack developer quietly drown in work outside their lane.
Can we replace the expert if the fit is wrong?
Yes. Within the first 14 days of the engagement, replacement is mutual and no-fault — no offboarding fee, no minimum commitment, no awkward conversation. After 14 days we will still replace, but it goes through a written review so we both learn from the mismatch.
Will the same expert stay across the engagement, or will you swap them out?
Same expert, by default. Swapping mid-engagement happens only with your written consent. The named Vybog tech lead behind the expert also stays the same — continuity is part of the contract, not a soft promise.
