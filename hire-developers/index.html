The category problem

What hiring through Vybog Services actually means.

Every category that exists for this — staff augmentation, dev shop, marketplace, agency — gets one part right and one part wrong. We have spent twenty-two years figuring out which parts to keep.

A marketplace will hand you a profile and walk away. The platform makes its money on the booking, not on the outcome. You will get the expert they had bench-time on, not the expert the work needed. When something goes sideways in week three, there is no one to call who owns the relationship.

A traditional staff-aug shop solves that — there is a person to call — but the experts themselves are often recruited the week your contract closes, with résumés that bear a polite distance from the work they have actually done. A different kind of disappointment with the same root cause: nobody is on the hook for the outcome.

Vybog Services is neither of those. The expert you hire is a full-time Vybog Services employee. They sit on our experting floor. A senior tech lead reviews their pull requests. We have known them for two years, three, sometimes ten. When we put them on your project, we are putting our reputation on the line — and our delivery lead is the person you can call when something feels off. That is the entire model.

Engagement Options

Buy by the hour, the month, or the milestone.

Three ways to engage a Vybog Services expert. The right one depends on how much certainty you have about the work, not how much capacity you want to commit.

Hourly

Pay by the hour

Time-tracked, invoiced weekly. Best for ambiguous scopes, support windows, code reviews, or filling a part-time gap on an existing team.

  • Minimum block: 20 hours per week
  • Weekly invoice with Harvest export
  • Cancel with one week of notice
Monthly

Dedicated by the month

One expert or a pod, full-time on your roadmap, fixed monthly rate. The model most clients move to after the first six weeks.

  • 40 hours per week, your standups, your repos
  • Predictable monthly burn, no overrun
  • 30-day notice to pause or resize
Project

Fixed scope, fixed price

A defined deliverable, an acceptance checklist, and a number. Milestone billing, one quarter of post-launch support included.

  • Written scope with sign-off criteria
  • Milestone-paid, no surprise change orders
  • 90 days of post-launch support included
7-Day Onboarding

From signed contract to first pull request in a week.

The onboarding sprint we run every time a new Vybog Services expert joins a client team. The dates are not aspirational — they are how we have been doing it for years.

D1

Paperwork closes

MSA, SOW, NDA and IP assignment signed. Expert gets your repo invite, Slack invite, and credentials checklist.

D2

Environment up

Local dev environment running, first build succeeds. Expert joins your Monday standup as a listener.

D3

Code-base read

Half a day of guided code walkthrough with your lead. Expert writes a one-page note on what they observed.

D5

First ticket merged

A small, low-risk ticket — a bug fix or a docs improvement — opened, reviewed, and merged by end of day.

D7

First feature underway

Expert picks up a real feature card. Vybog Services delivery lead checks in with both sides on the fit.

What's Included

What you get with every Vybog Services hire.

The contract baseline — what is always true, regardless of stack or engagement model.

  • NDA and IP assignment — signed before the expert sees your code. All work product is yours.
  • Source code in your repos — GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, self-hosted. We commit directly under your access policy.
  • Daily standup — async or synchronous, on your schedule, in your tool of choice.
  • Weekly demo — Friday afternoon, recorded, with a written summary the expert drafts before the call.
  • Monthly retro — Vybog Services delivery lead joins. What went well, what should change, what to drop.
  • 14-day mutual replacement window — if the fit is wrong in the first two weeks, we replace at no cost. After that, two weeks notice either way.
  • Tech lead oversight — every expert is backed by a senior Vybog Services tech lead reviewing PRs and unblocking when needed.
  • Status visibility — weekly hour summary, ticket throughput, blocker log. No mysteries on the invoice.

Frequently Asked

Common questions, before the discovery call.

How is this different from Toptal, Upwork, or a staffing agency?
Marketplaces sell access to freelancers and take a cut. Staffing agencies recruit to your request and re-sell those people. Vybog Services is neither — every expert we field is a full-time Vybog Services employee who has worked alongside our other experts for years, with our delivery lead accountable for the outcome. You are buying a relationship with Vybog Services, not a placement.
What time zones do your experts cover?
Default is four to six hours of live overlap with US Pacific or Eastern. Full overlap with UK, EU, Middle East, and APAC time zones. We have experts running US-Eastern hours on a sustained basis when needed — at a small premium reflecting the schedule.
What happens if the expert is not a fit?
For the first 14 days, either side can call it without charge — we replace immediately. After that, two weeks of written notice and we have someone else slotted in within a week, with no overlap fee. We have used this clause maybe a dozen times in twenty years; it is genuinely a safety net, not a marketing line.
Do you sign our MSA and security questionnaire?
Yes. Our standard contract works for most clients, but we sign client MSAs without friction. We complete security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, custom) inside one business week, and we hold SOC 2 Type II controls internally even though we do not publish the certification publicly.
Can a Vybog Services expert become a full-time hire on our team?
Yes, after twelve months on engagement. There is a placement fee that scales down by month worked, so the longer you have engaged with us the cheaper the conversion. We will not block the move — we would rather have you tell other founders we did right by you.
