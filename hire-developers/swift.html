De-Risk the Hire

The two-week discovery sprint, before commitment.

If you are bringing in a Swift developer for a long engagement, the worst time to find out the fit is wrong is week six. Here is how we shrink that risk.

The sprint is a fixed two-week, fixed-fee engagement where one of our senior Swift developers reads your codebase, joins your standups, and ships a small but real screen. At the end of the two weeks, both sides know enough to commit — or to walk away cleanly.

We bill the discovery sprint at a flat rate and credit it against the first month of any continuing engagement. If the fit is wrong, you keep the screen, the documentation, and the architectural notes the expert wrote up. Nobody loses the quarter.

Swift FAQ

Three questions we get on every Swift engagement.

Quick answers to what most clients ask. Reach out if you do not see your question.

SwiftUI or UIKit for a new app?
SwiftUI for new surfaces with iOS 17+ minimum. UIKit when the surface is heavy custom drawing or there are niche controls SwiftUI does not handle well. Mixed is normal — we will write the boundary rules.
React Native or Flutter when you have iOS experts on the bench?
Native iOS when the experience needs to feel native (gestures, motion, deep platform integration). React Native or Flutter when budget and time-to-market beat platform fidelity. We will tell you honestly which fits.
How do you handle App Review?
We read the guidelines every quarter, label privacy nutrition correctly, and submit with full screenshots and review notes. First-submission approval rate above 90%. We document the playbook for your team.
