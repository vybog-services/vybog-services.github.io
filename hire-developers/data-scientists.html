Specializations

Four tracks our Data Science bench is deep in.

Generalist Data Science is easy to find. These are the practices that separate a senior hire from a mid one.

Modeling, deployable

sklearn / PyTorch / XGBoost / LightGBM. The choice of model that actually fits the data, not the resume. Calibrated, validated, monitored.

Causal inference

When correlation will not cut it: DoWhy, EconML, propensity scoring, RCTs. We have shipped causal models in production for over two years.

MLOps from day one

MLflow for tracking, Vertex AI or SageMaker for serving, feature stores when they earn the complexity. Models in production with drift monitoring.

Experimentation

A/B tests with proper power calculations, holdout strategies, sequential testing. Statistically literate. Says "more data needed" when it is true.

The Senior Bar

What 'senior' actually means in our Data Science bench.

The five things every senior Data Science data scientist on our bench can be measured against. Not aspirational — these are the hiring rubric.

01

Model selection literacy

Picks the model the data fits, not the model the resume demands. Justifies tradeoffs in writing.

02

Validation discipline

Cross-validation, holdout, calibration, fairness. Knows when accuracy is hiding a bias problem.

03

Deploy bias

Cares about latency, memory, monitoring. Will not ship a model that cannot be served at the latency your product needs.

04

Engineering empathy

Reads code reviews. Pairs with data engineers on features. Knows the cost of every join.

05

Communication

Writes model cards. Explains the model to a non-technical PM. Says "we do not have enough data" when it is true.

De-Risk the Hire

The two-week discovery sprint, before commitment.

If you are bringing in a Data Science data scientist for a long engagement, the worst time to find out the fit is wrong is week six. Here is how we shrink that risk.

The sprint is a fixed two-week, fixed-fee engagement where one of our senior Data Science data scientists reads your codebase, joins your standups, and ships a small but real model. At the end of the two weeks, both sides know enough to commit — or to walk away cleanly.

We bill the discovery sprint at a flat rate and credit it against the first month of any continuing engagement. If the fit is wrong, you keep the model, the documentation, and the architectural notes the expert wrote up. Nobody loses the quarter.

Start a discovery sprint →

What the sprint covers

  • Days 1–3 · Audit data, current models, stakeholder interviews
  • Days 4–6 · First baseline model, one experiment scoped
  • Days 7–10 · One real model trained + validated + monitored
  • Days 11–14 · Deploy plan, next-quarter recommendations
  • End of sprint · Fit decision, transparent on both sides

Data Science FAQ

Three questions we get on every Data Science engagement.

Will your data scientists deploy the model themselves?
They will partner with MLOps / data engineering on the production path. They write the serving code, the monitoring config, and the rollback plan — but they will not single-handedly own infrastructure.
Causal inference — production-ready?
For uplift modeling, customer segmentation, and treatment effects, yes. For high-stakes safety / health decisions, we will tell you honestly when the assumptions do not hold.
GenAI / LLMs — do you do that work too?
Yes, but we sit it under our AI Agent Development service rather than under data science. Production-grade RAG, evals, observability. Reach out and we will point you at the right page.
