Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vybog Services right for early-stage teams without a CTO?
Yes. About a third of our engagements start before the client has a CTO. We give you the Vybog Services delivery lead as your interim experting accountability, and we'll happily hand off to your in-house CTO when you hire one.
How long is a typical engagement?
Hire-a-Developer averages 3-6 months and is often renewed. Dedicated pods average 6-18 months. Project engagements run 8-14 weeks. AI agent retainers are typically 12 months.
Do you offer a money-back option if it doesn't fit?
For Hire-a-Developer engagements: 14-day no-fault replacement, no offboarding fee. For project work: discovery-sprint refund within 14 days if you decide not to proceed.
What happens to my code at the end?
You own the code on full payment. All Vybog Services access is revoked within 30 days post-engagement, and you receive a written deletion certificate.
Can the AI agent run on-premise?
Yes. We have shipped agents that never call a public model API — running on a self-hosted Llama or Mistral behind your firewall. The trade-offs are spelled out plainly during discovery.
