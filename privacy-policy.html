1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vybog Services ("Vybog", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and safeguards personal information when you visit our website at vybogservices.com, contact our sales or hiring teams, engage us for consulting and engineering work, or interact with any digital surface we operate.

We operate two affiliated entities: Vybog Services, an Indian engineering studio headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and Vybog Inc., a US corporation headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Depending on where you are located and which entity you engage with, one or both entities may act as the controller of your data. Together they are referred to as "Vybog" in this policy.

This policy covers personal information of visitors to our websites, prospective and current clients, candidates applying to join Vybog, business contacts at our partners and vendors, and recipients of our marketing communications. It does not govern personal information that we process on behalf of our clients as a data processor — that processing is covered by the data protection terms in the relevant Statement of Work or Data Processing Agreement.

2.Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information. We collect only what is reasonably needed for the purposes described in Section 4.

2.1 Contact information

  • Name, business email, business phone number, job title, company name
  • Postal address (when you ask us to send physical materials or invoices)
  • Communication preferences and language

2.2 Technical information

  • IP address, approximate geolocation derived from IP, browser type and version
  • Operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Referring URL, pages visited, time spent on each page, links clicked
  • Session identifiers and cookie identifiers

2.3 Marketing information

  • Email open and click events from our newsletter and lifecycle emails
  • Form submissions (contact, quote request, newsletter signup, gated content downloads)
  • UTM parameters and campaign identifiers in URLs you arrive on

2.4 Usage data

  • How you interact with our marketing site (scroll depth, video plays, downloads)
  • For clients using Vybog-operated portals: login timestamps, feature usage, error reports

2.5 Employment information (candidates only)

  • CV / resume, cover letter, portfolio links, public code repository URLs
  • Salary expectations, notice period, work authorization status
  • References that you provide and interview notes generated by our hiring panel

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect special category data (race, religion, political opinion, health, sexual orientation, biometric identifiers, etc.) through this website. Please do not submit such information in forms or email unless we have explicitly asked for it and described the legal basis.

3.How We Collect It

We collect personal information through the following channels:

  • Directly from you — when you fill out a contact, quote, demo, newsletter, or careers form; when you email us; when you call us; when you exchange business cards or LinkedIn details with our team at events.
  • Automatically through cookies and similar technologies — when you browse our website. See Section 6.
  • Through analytics and product tools — such as Google Analytics, server logs, and product analytics on Vybog-operated portals.
  • From third parties — recruitment platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed) when you apply through them; sales intelligence providers (e.g., Apollo, ZoomInfo) where lawful; introducers and partners who refer you to us with your permission.
  • Through social logins — if you sign in to a Vybog-operated portal using a third-party identity provider (e.g., Google, Microsoft, GitHub), we receive the basic profile information that provider shares with us under your authorization.

4.How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

4.1 Service delivery

  • Respond to your enquiries, draft proposals, and prepare Statements of Work
  • Deliver the services you have engaged us for, including account management and project communications
  • Invoice you, collect payment, and maintain accounting records
  • Provide support, troubleshoot issues, and notify you of service-affecting events

4.2 Marketing communications

  • Send our newsletter, event invitations, and product or service announcements where you have opted in or where we have a legitimate interest as a B2B sender
  • Personalize the content we show on our website and in emails
  • Measure the performance of campaigns and adjust future outreach accordingly

4.3 Analytics and improvement

  • Understand which pages and content perform well and which need work
  • Detect and diagnose technical issues, bugs, and performance regressions
  • Improve the structure, navigation, and information architecture of our website

4.4 Recruiting

  • Evaluate applications, conduct interviews, and make hiring decisions
  • Maintain a talent pool for future roles, with your consent

4.5 Legal compliance and protection

  • Comply with tax, accounting, employment, anti-money-laundering, and other applicable laws
  • Respond to lawful requests from regulators, courts, and law enforcement
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

5.Legal Basis for Processing

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions, optional cookies, and any other processing where we explicitly ask you to opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the services you have engaged us for and to take pre-contractual steps at your request, such as preparing a proposal.
  • Legitimate interest — for business-to-business marketing to existing or prospective clients, fraud prevention, network and information security, and improving our services. We balance our interests against your rights and provide an easy way to object.
  • Legal obligation — when we must process information to comply with tax, accounting, employment, or other laws.

For California residents, the corresponding "business purposes" under the CCPA / CPRA include providing services, security, debugging, internal research, and quality control. We do not "sell" personal information as that term is defined under the CCPA, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising without your opt-in.

For residents of India, processing is performed in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") on the basis of your consent or for legitimate uses recognized under the Act.

6.Cookies and Tracking

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We use cookies and similar technologies (pixels, local storage, server logs) to operate the site, measure performance, and — only where you opt in — to personalize marketing.

6.1 Categories of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function, such as session management, security tokens, and remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand aggregate traffic and feature usage (e.g., Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity). Set only with your consent in jurisdictions that require it.
  • Marketing cookies — used to measure ad performance and serve relevant content on third-party platforms (e.g., LinkedIn Insight Tag, Meta Pixel). Set only with your consent.

6.2 How to control cookies

When you first visit our site from a jurisdiction that requires consent, you will see a cookie banner where you can accept, reject, or customize categories. You can change your choices at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in our footer, or by clearing cookies and revisiting the site. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although blocking essential cookies will break parts of the site.

7.Third-Party Sharing

We share personal information with the following categories of recipients, under written contracts that limit how they may use the data:

  • Subprocessors and infrastructure providers — cloud hosting (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud), email delivery (Postmark, Amazon SES), CRM (HubSpot), and similar operational vendors.
  • Analytics and marketing vendors — Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, LinkedIn, Meta, and similar tools — only to the extent enabled by your cookie choices.
  • Payment processors — for client invoicing, we use Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, and bank wire facilitators. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Professional advisors — accountants, auditors, lawyers, and insurers, bound by professional confidentiality.
  • Authorities and litigants — when required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Successors — if Vybog is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred to the acquiring party under equivalent protections.

We do not rent or sell personal information to third parties for their own marketing.

8.International Data Transfers

Because Vybog operates from India and the United States and serves clients globally, personal information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in India, the United States, and the European Economic Area, as well as other countries where our subprocessors operate.

Where personal information is transferred out of the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable) together with supplementary measures such as encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and contractual restrictions on government access requests.

For transfers from India, we comply with the cross-border transfer rules of the DPDP Act, including transfers only to countries that the Indian government has not restricted, and we maintain records of such transfers.

You may request a copy of the safeguards we use for international transfers by emailing privacy@vybogservices.com.

10.Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information.

10.1 For residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure — ask us to delete personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your information while we resolve a dispute
  • Portability — receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw any consent you previously gave, at any time
  • Lodge a complaint — file a complaint with your local data protection authority

10.2 For residents of California (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, and share
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell or share as defined under the CCPA)
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

10.3 For residents of India (DPDP Act)

  • Right to access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed
  • Right to correction and erasure of personal data that is inaccurate or no longer needed
  • Right of grievance redressal through our designated grievance officer
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity
  • Right to withdraw consent with the same ease as it was given

11.How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights above, email privacy@vybogservices.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and tell us which right you want to exercise. We will:

  1. Acknowledge your request within seven (7) calendar days.
  2. Verify your identity using the information we already hold about you. We may ask for additional verification if the request concerns sensitive data or a large data set.
  3. Respond substantively within 30 calendar days (GDPR), 45 calendar days (CCPA, extendable by another 45 days if necessary), or the period required by other applicable law. We will explain any delay.
  4. Provide our response free of charge, except where a request is manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive, in which case we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse the request.

You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We will require written proof of authorization and may verify identity directly with you.

12.Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at businesses and the professionals who run them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information about a child, please email privacy@vybogservices.com and we will delete it promptly.

13.Security Measures

We take security seriously and apply a defence-in-depth approach. Our controls include, without limitation:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher for all web traffic and API calls
  • Encryption at rest for databases, backups, and file storage using AES-256 or vendor equivalents
  • Access controls based on the principle of least privilege, with role-based permissions and multi-factor authentication for staff accounts
  • Network controls including private subnets, security groups, web application firewalls, and DDoS protection at the edge
  • Logging and monitoring of access to sensitive systems, with alerts for anomalous activity
  • Vendor due diligence on subprocessors, with data protection terms in every contract
  • Annual security training for all staff and additional role-specific training for engineers handling client data
  • Incident response plan with defined roles, escalation paths, and notification timelines aligned with GDPR's 72-hour breach notification requirement and the DPDP Act's reporting obligations

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We commit to taking the measures expected of a competent engineering firm and to notifying you and the relevant authorities promptly if a breach involving your personal information occurs.

14.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our services, or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. For material changes, we will provide reasonable advance notice by email (to newsletter subscribers and active clients) or by a prominent banner on the website. Continued use of our website or services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Earlier versions of this policy are available on request to privacy@vybogservices.com.
